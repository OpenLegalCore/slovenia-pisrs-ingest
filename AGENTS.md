# PISRS PIKI agent execution contract

Before work read [Master](docs/PIKI-PISRS-MASTER.md), [API](docs/PIKI-PISRS-API.md), [Environment](docs/PIKI-PISRS-ENV.md), [Data](docs/PIKI-PISRS-DATA.md), the [Linear project](https://linear.app/openlegalcore/project/pisrs-complete-source-to-index-synchronization-b47e7deb7c78) and [OLC-91](https://linear.app/openlegalcore/issue/OLC-91/pisrs-piki-g0-g2-credentials-uradni-openapi-in-ciljna-postgresql). Follow the linked architecture, synchronization, operations and acceptance contracts for design details.

- PIKI is new permanent staging. Medo is old production and read-only: a future second synchronized copy needs separate owner authorization. Do not modify Medo, Engine or case-law.
- G0–G2 require explicit final owner acceptance of one pinned documentation commit before the corresponding G3 installation/capture. Until then, authorized reads, documentation and design only; no setup, service actions, schema/data writes, ingestion, deployment or experimental branches. A later owner-approved G3 authorization permits only the staged source/PG work under the signed scope; G4 paid operation always needs its own measured token-cost approval.
- Official raw OpenAPI and redacted live evidence have different scopes. Preserve original source bytes in the design. Never infer catalogue, text or file identity or numeric coverage from counts. Record PASS, FAIL, NOT_VERIFIED or NOT_RUN with evidence and one next action.
- Keep project installs and data under `/data`, including Docker/PG/Qdrant storage, WAL, logs, cache, scratch and recovery. System disk holds source checkout and essential OS config.
- Keep tokens, DSNs, protected responses and private logs out of public artifacts and tool output. Use explicit service identities, redacted evidence and noninteractive no-pager reads. Never bypass privilege controls. No copying production secrets or private data.
- This is the single staging plan and review branch. Keep the existing main, Medo, Engine and case-law untouched. Require protected workflows and owner approval for any implementation baseline.
- Implement the single [Master](docs/PIKI-PISRS-MASTER.md#implementation-ready-specification-scope-authority-and-handoff), [Data](docs/PIKI-PISRS-DATA.md#binding-migration-provenance-and-view-rules), [Sync](docs/PIKI-PISRS-SYNC.md#deterministic-end-to-end-execution-contract-g3g5), [Operations](docs/PIKI-PISRS-OPERATIONS.md#end-to-end-release-and-operational-command-contract) and [Acceptance](docs/PIKI-PISRS-ACCEPTANCE.md#execution-to-acceptance-traceability-and-signoff-rules) contracts. The owner confirms the valid 2024 PISRS permit and successful PIKI token; NEVER require new SVZ permission. Apply configurable conservative pacing with 429/Retry-After and honest incomplete states; do not invent numerical quotas. Independent backup destination is UNDECIDED and deferred to [OLC-94](https://linear.app/openlegalcore/issue/OLC-94) after verified sync on both PIKI and separately upgraded Medo; do not block G2/G3 with it or mislabel a local capture as independent backup. G1 owner option A positive cases are evidence, B UNRESOLVED/AMBIGUOUS remains mandatory fail-closed behavior. No email without owner confirmation; do not rerun historical API pilots.
